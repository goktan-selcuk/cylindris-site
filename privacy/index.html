English

Cylindris is a mobile game developed by Göktan Selçuk. This policy explains what data the game processes.

No data collected. Cylindris does not collect, store or share any personal data. The game does not require an internet connection; scores, settings and progress stay on your device.

Game Center. You may use Apple's Game Center for leaderboards. Signing in is optional. Your scores and Game Center nickname are processed by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; the developer has no access to this data beyond the leaderboard Apple provides. See https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

No ads, no tracking. The game shows no ads, does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not track you.

Children. Since no personal data is collected, the game is suitable for all ages.

Changes. If this policy changes, this page will be updated.
